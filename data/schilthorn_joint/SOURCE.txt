Schilthorn (Swiss Alps) co-located ERT + seismic-refraction permafrost profile
================================================================================

A small redistributed extract of the co-located electrical-resistivity-tomography
(ERT) and seismic-refraction-tomography (SRT) profile across the Schilthorn
permafrost monitoring site (Bernese Alps, Switzerland, ~2900 m a.s.l.), used here
for a joint (cross-gradient) inversion teaching notebook.

Files
-----
  ert.data   pyGIMLi "unified data format": 50 electrodes + 358 quadrupoles
             (columns a b m n u i err k rhoa; rhoa = apparent resistivity in Ohm.m,
             err = relative error).
  rst.data   pyGIMLi unified format: 52 sensors + 620 first-arrival traveltimes
             (columns s g t err; t = first-arrival time in seconds, err absolute).

Primary method / data reference
-------------------------------
  Wagner, F. M., Mollaret, C., Guenther, T., Kemna, A., & Hauck, C. (2019).
  Quantitative imaging of water, ice and air in permafrost systems through
  petrophysical joint inversion of seismic refraction and electrical resistivity
  data. Geophysical Journal International, 219(3), 1866-1875.
  https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggz402   (CC-BY 4.0)

  Reference implementation (BSD-3-Clause):
  https://github.com/florian-wagner/four-phase-inversion

Field data / monitoring network
--------------------------------
  PERMOS 2019. PERMOS Database. Swiss Permafrost Monitoring Network, Fribourg and
  Davos, Switzerland. https://doi.org/10.13093/permos-2019-01

  Site soil-moisture context:
  Pellet, C., Hilbich, C., Marmy, A., & Hauck, C. (2016). Soil Moisture Data for
  the Validation of Permafrost Models Using Direct and Indirect Measurement
  Approaches at Three Alpine Sites. Frontiers in Earth Science, 3, 91.
  https://doi.org/10.3389/feart.2015.00091
