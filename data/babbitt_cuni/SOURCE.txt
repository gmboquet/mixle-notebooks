Babbitt Cu-Ni deposit drill-hole dataset (Duluth Complex, Minnesota, USA)
========================================================================

Files
-----
collar_BABBITT.csv   399 drill-hole collars: BHID, XCOLLAR, YCOLLAR, ZCOLLAR
survey_BABBITT.csv   down-hole surveys:     BHID, AT, AZ, DIP
assay_BABBITT.csv    35,616 assay intervals: BHID, FROM, TO, CU, NI, S, FE

Units & conventions
-------------------
- Coordinates and depths are in FEET (a US state-plane / local mine grid),
  NOT metres.
- CU, NI, S are in weight percent (%); FE is sparse (only ~24 values) and is
  effectively unusable.
- Blank assay cells mean "not assayed" (barren / un-sampled rock) and are
  NA, NOT zero. They must be dropped, not filled with 0.
- Survey DIP is measured from horizontal (90 = vertical down); AZ is the
  down-hole azimuth in degrees.
- Collar BHID 34873 sits ~6,000 ft off the main drill grid and is a deep
  vertical reference/condemnation hole; exclude it for grid-scale work.
