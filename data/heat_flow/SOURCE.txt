IHFC Global Heat Flow Database — Release 2024 (extract)

ihfc_us_onshore.csv is a filtered extract (US onshore-continental wells carrying heat flow + temperature
gradient + thermal conductivity, clamped to physical bounds) of:

    Global Heat Flow Data Assessment Group, Fuchs, S., Norden, B., et al. (2024).
    The Global Heat Flow Database: Release 2024. GFZ Data Services.
    https://doi.org/10.5880/fidgeo.2024.014
